# The Future of Multi-modal AI in Medicine

Multi-modal AI is revolutionizing healthcare by combining disparate data sources like medical images (radiology, pathology) with clinical text and genomic data.

My work explores these intersections across medical imaging, clinical language, and production AI systems.
